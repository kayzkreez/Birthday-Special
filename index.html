My dearest Tadisa, On this day, the world was given someone wonderfully kind, strong, and full of light. You are my beginning, my middle, and my end. Happy birthday, my love. This is one little piece of the life I hope we keep building together. And yes: I love you be be be be cccccccauasee… there are simply too many reasons, and my heart gets stuck trying to say them all.

Always yours,
