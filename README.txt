52 REASONS WHY YOU'RE MY FOREVER — QUICK START

1. Open index.html in a browser to preview the site.
2. Upload the whole folder to any static website host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, or Vercel).
3. Countdown is set to 10 October 2026 at 00:00 in Zimbabwe time (UTC+2).

PERSONAL MEDIA
Put your files inside the assets folder with these exact names:
- our-song.mp3       Your chosen song (the browser may require a tap before music can play).
- kuda-message.mp4   Your birthday video message.
- photo-1.jpg       First couple photo.
- photo-2.jpg       Second couple photo.
- photo-3.jpg       Third couple photo.
- photo-4.jpg       Fourth couple photo.
- photo-5.jpg       Fifth couple photo.

The page works without these media files; the relevant areas will simply have no media to show.

EDITING
Open index.html in a text editor. Search for “Tadisa” or “Kudakwashe” to change the names and search for “const TARGET” to change the countdown. The 52 reasons are in the JavaScript array named “reasons”.

NOTES
- The countdown target is fixed to the upcoming birthday date based on the known birthday, 10 October.
- The page is static and does not collect or transmit personal data.
- Audio autoplay is controlled by the browser and may be blocked until the visitor interacts with the page.
